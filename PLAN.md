# Influencer Social Ops Layer

## Features Plan

> A lightweight, tools-based social media operations platform for influencers and their small teams.
> Minimal UI • Automation-first • AI-assisted • Fair pricing

---

## Vision

Build a modern layer for social media influencers that combines:

- Unified inbox & engagement
- Cross-platform publishing
- Smart automation
- Team collaboration
- AI assistance
- Analytics & moderation

**Positioning:**
The operating system for influencer teams — simpler than agency tools, more powerful than basic creator tools.

---

## Target Users

### Primary

- Mid-size influencers (10k–500k+) who manage multiple platforms
- Influencers with 1–5 team members (manager, VA, content creator)

### Secondary

- Small creator agencies
- Talent managers handling a few creators

---

## Full Feature Set

### Unified Social Inbox

- Aggregate comments, DMs, mentions
- Reply from one dashboard
- Assign conversations to team members
- Internal notes and @mentions

### Cross-Platform Publishing

- Post to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.
- Schedule posts
- Content calendar
- Platform-specific captions & formats

### Cross-Platform Analytics

- Followers, reach, engagement, views
- Post performance
- Platform comparison
- Team and response analytics

### Automation Engine

- Keyword comment → auto DM
- Auto replies
- Event-based automations
- Smart routing to sales/support/team
- Moderation rules

### Team Management

- Create teams
- Roles and permissions
- Assign posts / conversations
- Workload tracking
- Audit logs

### Approval Workflows

- Post approval
- Comment / reply approval
- Manager review
- Approve / reject / edit before publishing

### AI Assistant

- AI reply suggestions
- Caption generation
- Conversation summaries
- Content repurposing
- Brand-tone controls

### Social Listening

- Brand mentions
- Keyword / hashtag tracking
- Competitor monitoring
- Sentiment analysis
- Crisis / spike alerts

### Content Management

- Asset / media library
- Drafts
- Campaign folders
- Saved captions
- Saved replies / templates

### Moderation

- Spam detection
- Hide / delete comments
- Block keywords
- Flag abusive content
- Escalate sensitive messages

### Campaign Management

- Group posts under campaigns
- Campaign calendar
- Campaign analytics
- Track performance across platforms

### Notifications & Alerts

- New assigned conversation
- Missed reply / SLA alert
- Viral post alert
- Negative sentiment spike
- Approval notification

## MVP Scope

### Platforms

- Meta (Instagram + Facebook)
- TikTok
- YouTube

### MVP Features

- User authentication + team creation
- Connect accounts via OAuth (user has full control)
- Cross-platform publishing
- Content calendar + scheduling
- Platform-specific captions
- Media / asset library + drafts
- Basic post performance analytics
- Simple unified view of comments
- Reply to comments from dashboard
- Meta DMs (where available)
- Assign posts or conversations to team members
- Basic post approval workflow
- AI caption generation
- AI reply suggestions (with basic brand tone)
- Notifications for assignments and approvals

### Nice-to-have in MVP

- Saved replies / templates
- Simple keyword block list
- Basic campaign grouping

### Out of MVP

- X / LinkedIn / other platforms
- Full social listening
- Advanced automation engine
- Deep moderation / spam detection
- Complex multi-level approvals
- Competitor monitoring
- Advanced team analytics
